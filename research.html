Research

Research Profile

My research objective is to apply Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques to solve real-world problems in education, healthcare, and societal development. My areas of interest include Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, Large Language Models, and Quantum Computing.

Research Projects

Research projects
Sr. No.ProjectObjectiveOutcome
1Adapting LLM for Regional Language ProcessingLLM fine-tuning, Marathi languageTo adapt Large Language Models (LLMs) through fine-tuning on task-specific and language-specific datasets, enhancing their ability to handle cultural context, linguistic diversity, and domain-specific nuances of the Marathi language.Developed datasets for three tasks and three specialized models: Marathi_Para, Marathi_Class, and Marathi_Senti. They significantly outperformed the base model in both quantitative metrics and qualitative measures such as fluency, relevance, and correctness. The models reached 90% accuracy in classification and 70% in sentiment analysis.
2Adapting LLM for Question Paper GenerationRetrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Bloom’s TaxonomyTo utilize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to integrate LLMs with external academic course materials, ensuring that generated question papers are contextually relevant, syllabus-aligned, and pedagogically sound according to Bloom’s Taxonomy.Developed an AI-based system that demonstrated higher alignment with course objectives and improved syllabus coverage compared to generic models, producing structured, diverse, and ready-to-use examination papers.
3Adapting LLM for Student Assessment EvaluationOpenAI ADA-002 embeddings, cosine similarityTo adapt LLM embeddings (specifically OpenAI ADA-002) and cosine similarity to create semantic representations of student answers and compare them with model answers for fair, transparent, and scalable automated grading.Developed a system that evaluates descriptive answers with high accuracy, closely matching subject-expert grading with low Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), while reducing bias and human error in the evaluation process.
4Adapting LLM for Student Support SystemGemini 1.5 Pro, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)To enhance LLM capabilities (using Gemini 1.5 Pro) by integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with a university-specific knowledge base to provide precise, context-aware, and multilingual administrative support.Developed an advanced chatbot that outperformed generic models in accuracy and relevance, significantly reducing hallucinations by retrieving information directly from authenticated university documents.
