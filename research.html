Adapting LLM for Regional Language Processing

Objective

To adapt Large Language Models (LLMs) through fine-tuning on task-specific and language-specific datasets, enhancing their ability to handle cultural context, linguistic diversity, and domain-specific nuances of the Marathi language.

How it works

  1. Build datasets for three Marathi language tasks.
  2. Fine-tune the base LLM on each dataset to create a specialized model for the task.
  3. Compare every specialized model with the base model on quantitative metrics and on fluency, relevance, and correctness.

Outcome

Developed datasets for three tasks and three specialized models: Marathi_Para, Marathi_Class, and Marathi_Senti. They significantly outperformed the base model in both quantitative metrics and qualitative measures such as fluency, relevance, and correctness.

  • 90% accuracy for classification
  • 70% accuracy for sentiment

Adapting LLM for Question Paper Generation

Objective

To utilize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to integrate LLMs with external academic course materials, ensuring that generated question papers are contextually relevant, syllabus-aligned, and pedagogically sound according to Bloom’s Taxonomy.

How it works

  1. Provide the LLM with external academic course materials through retrieval.
  2. Generate questions grounded in the retrieved syllabus content and aligned to Bloom’s Taxonomy.
  3. Assemble the questions into structured, diverse, and ready-to-use examination papers.

Outcome

Developed an AI-based system that demonstrated higher alignment with course objectives and improved syllabus coverage compared to generic models, producing structured, diverse, and ready-to-use examination papers.

Adapting LLM for Student Assessment Evaluation

Objective

To adapt LLM embeddings (specifically OpenAI ADA-002) and cosine similarity to create semantic representations of student answers and compare them with model answers for fair, transparent, and scalable automated grading.

How it works

  1. Convert each student answer and the model answer into ADA-002 embeddings.
  2. Measure how close the two are in meaning using cosine similarity.
  3. Convert the similarity into marks for descriptive answers.
  4. Validate the marks against subject-expert grading using Root Mean Square Error (RMSE).

Outcome

Developed a system that evaluates descriptive answers with high accuracy, closely matching subject-expert grading with low Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), while reducing bias and human error in the evaluation process.

Adapting LLM for Student Support System

Objective

To enhance LLM capabilities (using Gemini 1.5 Pro) by integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with a university-specific knowledge base to provide precise, context-aware, and multilingual administrative support.

How it works

  1. Build a knowledge base from authenticated university documents.
  2. Retrieve the passages relevant to each student query.
  3. Let Gemini 1.5 Pro answer from the retrieved text, in the student’s language.
  4. Compare answers with generic models for accuracy, relevance, and hallucination.

Outcome

Developed an advanced chatbot that outperformed generic models in accuracy and relevance, significantly reducing hallucinations by retrieving information directly from authenticated university documents.

EEG-Based Analysis of Learning and Yoga-Induced Stress Reduction

Objective

To use brain signals recorded with Electroencephalography (EEG) to understand how students learn, from their level of understanding and concentration to the effect of yoga and meditation on stress.

How it works

  1. Record EEG signals from participants during learning and cognitive activities.
  2. Train machine learning and deep learning models to predict understanding and concentration.
  3. Review the EEG literature on yoga and meditation, then design a standardized system that quantifies cumulative stress reduction using adaptive Independent Component Analysis (ICA).

Outcome

A study on students of different ages and educational backgrounds found that KNN and SVM predicted the level of understanding with nearly identical accuracy. The work was extended to concentration prediction with deep learning.

  • About 99% accuracy with KNN and SVM

Quantum Computing and Hybrid Quantum-Classical Software

Objective

To learn, teach, and build for quantum computing, including how to design software that combines quantum and classical components in one system.

How it works

  1. Study the foundations of quantum computing and work hands-on with Qiskit.
  2. Define the components a hybrid quantum-classical system needs.
  3. Organize them into a framework that guides how such systems are designed.

Outcome

Proposed the Quantum Software Architecture Framework (QSAF), a component-based framework for designing hybrid quantum-classical systems.
