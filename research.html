Research

Applying Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to real-world problems in education, healthcare, and societal development.

Research profile

My research objective is to apply Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques to solve real-world problems in education, healthcare, and societal development. My areas of interest include Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, Large Language Models, and Quantum Computing.

Research projects

Adapting LLM for Regional Language ProcessingNatural Language Processing

Objective. To adapt Large Language Models (LLMs) through fine-tuning on task-specific and language-specific datasets, enhancing their ability to handle cultural context, linguistic diversity, and domain-specific nuances of the Marathi language.

Outcome. Developed datasets for three tasks and three specialized models: Marathi_Para, Marathi_Class, and Marathi_Senti. They significantly outperformed the base model in both quantitative metrics and qualitative measures such as fluency, relevance, and correctness.

  • 90% accuracy for classification
  • 70% accuracy for sentiment
  • LLM fine-tuning
  • Marathi language
  • Task-specific datasets
Adapting LLM for Question Paper GenerationEducational technology

Objective. To utilize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to integrate LLMs with external academic course materials, ensuring that generated question papers are contextually relevant, syllabus-aligned, and pedagogically sound according to Bloom’s Taxonomy.

Outcome. Developed an AI-based system that demonstrated higher alignment with course objectives and improved syllabus coverage compared to generic models, producing structured, diverse, and ready-to-use examination papers.

  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • Large Language Models
  • Bloom’s Taxonomy
Adapting LLM for Student Assessment EvaluationEducational technology

Objective. To adapt LLM embeddings (specifically OpenAI ADA-002) and cosine similarity to create semantic representations of student answers and compare them with model answers for fair, transparent, and scalable automated grading.

Outcome. Developed a system that evaluates descriptive answers with high accuracy, closely matching subject-expert grading with low Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), while reducing bias and human error in the evaluation process.

  • OpenAI ADA-002 embeddings
  • Cosine similarity
  • Automated grading
Adapting LLM for Student Support SystemEducational technology

Objective. To enhance LLM capabilities (using Gemini 1.5 Pro) by integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with a university-specific knowledge base to provide precise, context-aware, and multilingual administrative support.

Outcome. Developed an advanced chatbot that outperformed generic models in accuracy and relevance, significantly reducing hallucinations by retrieving information directly from authenticated university documents.

  • Gemini 1.5 Pro
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • University knowledge base
  • Multilingual chatbot
EEG-Based Analysis of Learning and Yoga-Induced Stress ReductionNeurophysiological signals and machine learning

Objective. To use brain signals recorded with Electroencephalography (EEG) to understand how students learn, from their level of understanding and concentration to the effect of yoga and meditation on stress.

Outcome. A study on students of different ages and educational backgrounds found that KNN and SVM predicted the level of understanding with nearly identical accuracy. The work was extended to concentration prediction with deep learning.

  • About 99% accuracy with KNN and SVM
  • EEG signals
  • Machine learning (KNN, SVM)
  • Deep learning
  • Adaptive ICA
Quantum Computing and Hybrid Quantum-Classical SoftwareQuantum computing

Objective. To learn, teach, and build for quantum computing, including how to design software that combines quantum and classical components in one system.

Outcome. Proposed the Quantum Software Architecture Framework (QSAF), a component-based framework for designing hybrid quantum-classical systems.

  • Qiskit
  • IBM Quantum
  • Software architecture
  • Hybrid quantum-classical systems
Applied Machine Learning for Agriculture and HealthcareApplied machine learning

Objective. To apply machine learning to practical problems in society, such as predicting suitable crops for farmers and detecting COVID-19.

Outcome. A comparative performance analysis of machine learning models for agricultural crop prediction, and a study of deep learning techniques for detecting COVID-19.

  • Machine learning
  • Deep learning
  • Comparative analysis
